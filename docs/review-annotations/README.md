# Review Annotations

The Review Annotations feature is a built-in tool for leaving review notes directly on prototype screens. It is designed for UX designers, design leads, and PMs who want to annotate screens during review sessions without leaving the prototype.

---

## What It Is

Review Annotations is a side panel that appears when you click the "Review" button in the app header. It shows review notes (called annotations) for the screen you are currently viewing. Notes are attached to specific pages — when you navigate to a different screen, the panel updates to show only the notes for that screen.

This is a reviewer utility. It is not part of the product flow that end users see. It is only visible when Review Mode is active.

---

## How to Open It

Click the **Review** button in the top header of the app. The annotation panel slides open on the right side of the screen. To close it, click the Review button again.

---

## The Three Severity Levels

Every annotation has a severity level:

| Level | Icon / Label | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Note** | Info | General observations, suggestions, or questions that are not blockers. Copy improvements, design preferences, or things to consider. |
| **Warning** | Warning | A concern that should be addressed before the screen is considered done. Causes friction or missing states, but does not block users. |
| **Critical** | Critical | A must-fix issue. A real user would be blocked, confused, or could lose data. Blocks review sign-off until resolved. |

---

## Page-Aware Behavior

The annotation panel is page-aware. It shows only the notes associated with the screen you are currently viewing. Navigate to a different page and the panel automatically refreshes to show that page's notes.

This makes it easy to review one screen at a time without annotations from other screens getting in the way.
